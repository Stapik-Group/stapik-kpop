# Getting started

Stapik K-Pop keeps your K-pop collection in one place: albums, photocards, merchandise, clips, lyrics and events you
went to. Everything is stored on your computer, and you can optionally synchronise it with your own Stapik Cloud
(see [Cloud synchronisation](#cloud)).

## The main window

- On the left is the list of kinds: **All**, **Albums**, **Photocards**, **Merchandise**, **Clips**, **Lyrics** and
  **Events**. Click one to see only that kind.
- At the top there is the search field, the filters for status and artist and the sort order.
- In the middle is the list of entries, shown as a **List** or as a **Shelf** of covers.
- At the bottom you see how many entries you have, how many copies you own and how much you spent, and, when the cloud
  is connected, the state of the synchronisation.

## Your first entry

1. Choose **Collection → Add entry…** (or press Ctrl+N, or click **Add entry** in an empty list).
2. Pick the **Type**, for example *Albums*, and write the **Title**.
3. Choose the **Artist**. If it is not on the list yet, add it first, see [Artists](#artists).
4. Fill in what you know. Only the title is required.
5. Confirm. The entry is saved at once.

> Every change is saved automatically. There is no Save button. If something goes wrong, use **Edit → Undo**
> (Ctrl+Z) or restore an older copy, see [Backups](#backups).

Press F1 at any time to open this tutorial again.
