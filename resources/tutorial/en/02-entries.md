# Entries

An entry is one thing in your collection. There are six kinds, and each has its own fields in addition to the common ones.

## Common fields

- **Title** and **Artist**.
- **Cover / photo** and **Additional photos**, see [Covers and photos](#images).
- **Status**: *Owned*, *Ordered*, *Wishlist* or *Sold*.
- **Condition**: *Not specified*, *Sealed*, *Mint*, *Good*, *Fair* or *Poor*.
- **Copies**: how many of it you have.
- **Purchase price** and its currency, **Acquired on** and **Acquired from**.
- **Notes**.

Dates may be partial. You can write only a year (`2021`), a year and a month (`2021-08`) or a full date (`2021-08-23`).

Amounts are read the way your language writes them, so `12,50`, `12.50` and `1 234,56` all work. A field with text
that cannot be understood is marked, and the entry cannot be confirmed until you fix it.

## Kinds of entries

- **Albums**: album type (single, mini, full, repackage…), format (CD, vinyl, cassette, digital, kit…), edition,
  variant, release date, label, region, catalog number, inclusions and the **tracklist** with titles, writers, length
  and the title track.
- **Photocards**: member, origin (album inclusion, pre-order benefit, lucky draw, fansign, event), source and whether it
  is available for trade.
- **Merchandise**: kind of item (lightstick, apparel, photobook, season's greetings…), size, version and whether it is
  official.
- **Clips**: clip type, platform, link, release date, the album it belongs to and whether you have watched it.
- **Lyrics**: the album, writers, the original text, romanization and a translation with its language.
- **Events**: event type (concert, fansign, fan meeting…), date, venue, city and seat.

## Changing entries

- Double-click an entry, or choose **Edit** from its context menu (right-click), to change it.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy with a new identity, **Duplicate and edit…** opens the copy at once. It is handy for
  several versions of one album.
- **Delete** asks for confirmation. In the shelf view you can also select an entry and press the Delete key.
- **Edit → Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z) work for every change, including deleting.
