# Artists

Every entry belongs to an artist. Open the list with **Collection → Artists…**.

- **Add** creates an artist. Give a **Name**, choose the **Type** (*Group* or *Soloist*) and, for a group, write the
  **Members**, one per line.
- **Edit** (or a double-click) changes an artist. Members are used for photocards and by the search.
- **Delete** removes an artist, but only one that has no entries. Otherwise you are told how many entries still use it.
  Delete or reassign them first.

Each artist gets its own colour. In the list and in the grid the name of the artist is shown as a small tag with a dot in that colour.
