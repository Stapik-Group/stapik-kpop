# Finding and sorting

## Kinds, statuses and artists

Click a kind on the left to narrow the list. The two lists at the top narrow it further to a **status** (for example
only *Wishlist*) and to one **artist**. The filters work together.

## Search

Type in the search field. The program looks in titles, artists and their members, notes, the places you bought things
at, tracklists, writers, lyrics (original, romanization and translation), clip and event details.

- Upper and lower case do not matter, and neither do accents: `lodz` finds *Łódź*.
- Korean and Japanese work too. A beginning of a word is enough, for example `방탄` finds `방탄소년단`.
- When you write several words, an entry has to contain all of them.
- Enter searches at once, Esc clears the field.

## Sorting

The last list at the top sets the order: recently or oldest added, title, artist or release date. Titles and artists
are put in alphabetical order of the language of the program, so in Polish *Ł* comes after *L*.

## List or shelf

Use **List** and **Shelf** to switch the view. The shelf shows covers, which is nicer for albums and photocards. Long
lists are split into pages of 50 entries, with buttons below.

When nothing matches, the window says so. Change the search text or the filters.
